The same six renders, coloured two ways. bands is what dadaspec ships: three hard frequency bands, red / green / blue, mixed by energy. ramp gives every mel bin its own hue and mixes those instead.

The split is lopsided: 200 Hz sits at 6% of the mel scale and 1500 Hz at 38%, so red owns 6% of the range and blue 62%. Yellow and cyan only ever appear where two bands overlap, which is why they read as narrow seams rather than part of the scale.

Both columns are 1600×200 at 100 mel bins, same audio, same brightness. Only the hue mapping differs.
